Hey, I’m Aliah!

From the raw intensity of metal to the precariously trained runs of RnB, I’m your girl. A vocalist, guitarist, and a regular face in the indie scene since 2024, I’ve opened for countless shows, notably, Villain Era by Shrirupa Sengupta which features an all jazz and soft rock set, taken my own fronted band to countless competitions including IIT-Madras.

My sound

Specialized in RnB, vocal runs, falsetto flips, and of course, belting is second nature to me. With my experience in metal, training in vocal distortion for the same, both these genres combined give me an intense yet refined edge to my music through sheer power and years of developing my range and vocal technicalities.
